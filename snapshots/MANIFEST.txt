Subject snapshot manifest
=========================

Every subject is pinned to an exact commit. The human baselines are cloned from
their public repositories at the tags/SHAs below. The 15 agentic subjects are
frozen at the final commit of their generation run and are committed inside
this repository under subjects/<run id>/, together with their traces under
generation/runs/<run id>/: they are the output of stochastic runs against
hosted models and cannot be regenerated identically, so they are shipped rather
than reproduced.

Agentic subjects
----------------
Generated 2026-10-14 (UTC) by generation/run_generation.py, five runs per
configuration under one budget: 90 min wall-clock, 500 tool calls, temperature
0.2, and a $10 per-run cost cap. generation/runs/MANIFEST.json records each
run's start and end time, wall-clock, exit status and frozen commit; the
per-run cost and model-call counts are in each SWE-agent run's trajectory
(generation/runs/<id>/*/*.traj.gz) and are aggregated by
analysis/paper_numbers.py.

  Analysis scope, for every agentic run, is the framework package the agent
  created, declared per run as "include" in subjects/subjects.json. Test
  files, example applications and scratch scripts left outside that package
  are excluded, so each subject is measured on its implementation code only.
  swe_django_run05 also left a tests/ package behind; it is outside the scope
  for the same reason.

  All 18 subjects are registered `available: true`. Two runs ended at the
  cost cap rather than at the agent's own submission (swe_flask_run03 and
  swe_django_run05); the trajectories record the exit status.

Analysis toolchain
------------------
Pinned in requirements-analysis.txt (Radon, complexipy, Bandit, pylint, pytest,
SciPy). The snapshots' own runtime dependencies are pinned separately in
requirements-subjects.txt and must be installed, or neither pylint nor the
oracle can resolve a subject's imports. CPD ships with PMD 7.0.0, installed in
the Dockerfile.

All of results/ and logs/ is produced by the hermetic image
(make docker && make docker-run); logs/environment.txt records the interpreter,
the resolved tool versions, PMD/JRE versions, and the determinism check. Two
independent runs give byte-identical JSON and CSV.
